# App artwork

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Original generated PNGs are retained here; resized PNG/ICO deliverables are used by the Windows app, browser favicon, and Fire TV launcher.

- `../../build/icon.ico`: Windows app icon (16–256 px).
- `../../public/favicon.ico`: browser favicon (16–64 px).
- `../../public/favicon-32.png`: 32 px favicon.
- `../../public/app-icon.png`: 512 px app icon.
- `../../firetv/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/iptv_launcher.png`: Fire TV icon.
- `../../firetv/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/iptv_banner.png`: 320×180 TV launcher banner.

## Final icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square app icon for an IPTV player and matching small favicon. Create one polished flat graphic: a bold rounded television screen outline enclosing a right-facing play triangle. Match the existing app's midnight navy background and bright blue focus accents, with a crisp white play symbol. Center the mark, generous safe margins, simple thick geometry readable at 16 pixels. Full-bleed solid midnight navy square background, no text, no letters, no gradients, no texture, no mockup or presentation sheet. One icon only.

## Final banner prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Make a landscape 16:9 Fire TV launcher banner, matching the attached square IPTV app icon exactly in symbol and colors. Midnight navy full bleed background. Place the same bold blue television and white play symbol on the left, with large clean white sans serif text 'IPTV' on the right. Balanced centered composition, generous outer safe margins. Flat crisp graphic, no tiny text, no mockup, no frame. One landscape banner only.
